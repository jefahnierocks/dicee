SAFETY
  The Copilot wrapper intentionally exposes no deploy, secret-change,
  migration, production-log, or provider-mutation commands.

  Do not treat package deploy scripts as routine agent commands.
  The manually dispatched GitHub workflow is the canonical production path,
  and live operations require explicit operator authority.
